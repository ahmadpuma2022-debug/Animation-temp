# What if you floated up and couldn't stop? — journey (26 stages, 9:00)

YouTube check: the most-viewed video with this exact idea has 2,461 views ([The Cosmic Witness](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbZqpLaJiRI)); the rest are under 1,000. The closest bigger video is a different idea (Minute Zero Extra, "Got Lighter", 25,896 views).

Staging: the landmarks (Eiffel Tower, Burj Khalifa, Everest) stand beside you at their true heights, the way scale-comparison videos do. Set pieces in space (plane, balloon, ISS, GPS satellite, Neptune, Voyager 1) are staged to pass close by you; the number is always the real one.

Top-right number: outside temperature (standard atmosphere) up to 86 km; it holds the 86 km value until it switches to gravity at ~95 km (just before the Kármán line, stage 17).

## Checks (recomputed during the build, 2026-11-08)
- Temperatures and pressures, stages 1–16: recomputed with the 1976 standard atmosphere (geopotential height). All match to 0.1 °C. Stage 15 is −18.9 °C (was rounded to −19).
- Gravity, stages 17–26: 9.80665 × (6,371 ÷ (6,371 + h))². All match.
- Light times: Moon 1.28 s, 1 AU 8 min 19 s, Neptune 4 h 10 min. **Corrected:** 6.0 billion km is 5 h 34 min (was "about 5 h 37 min"). One light-day = 25,902,068,371 km.
- Stage 22: the Moon passes 12,000 km to your side, so the closest pass is at 377,842 km altitude (384,400 km from Earth's centre). It fills about 16 degrees of your view and is on screen 6:51.5–6:58.6.
- Stage 1: changed "kitchen" to your room: the engine's room is a bedroom. The line doesn't mention the room.

## Estimates and weak points
- Ceiling height (stage 2), roof height (stage 3), cloud base (stage 7), balloon burst height (stage 14) and the ISS's "about 400 km" are estimates.
- Temperatures are for a standard day; a real day can differ by 10–20 °C in the lower layers.
- At stage 23 you leave straight up out of the orbital plane, so you don't fly into the Sun.
- Voyager 1 reaches one light-day on 18 Nov 2026, so stage 26 is accurate only from that date. Don't publish before then.
